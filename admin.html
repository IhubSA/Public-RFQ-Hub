Your information is protected

This admin console processes personal information relating to tender applicants and staff — including names, contact details, submitted documents, and records of decisions and system activity.

All information is handled in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Access is restricted to authorised staff, and every action in this system is logged for accountability.
